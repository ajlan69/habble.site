Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 January 2026

HABBLE is an independent one-person web-design service, so this policy is short and specific rather than long and vague. It covers this website and the projects we build for clients. If something is not covered here, ask us and we will answer in writing.

1. What we collect

  • Information you send us — your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, social handles, and the project details you write in the contact form or send by message.
  • Content for your project — text, images, résumés, project files and links you provide for the site we build.
  • Access details you share — for example a Cloudflare account invitation or a domain registrar login, so we can connect and deploy your site. We ask for the minimum access needed and never for payment card details.

2. What this website collects

This site is static HTML, CSS and JavaScript. There is no analytics script, no advertising pixel, no cookie banner and no tracking of your browsing on this page.

  • The contact form does not send anything to a server. It builds a message and hands it to your own email app, so the message travels directly from you to us through your mail provider.
  • Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means Google’s servers see your IP address when the page loads. You can block this in your browser or ad blocker; the page falls back to system fonts.
  • Our host (GitHub Pages or Cloudflare Pages) keeps standard server logs such as IP address, browser type and requested page. Those are governed by their own privacy policies.

3. Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and send the quote you asked for.
  • To build, maintain and support your website.
  • To send invoices and payment reminders, and to keep records for tax and accounting.
  • To meet legal obligations that apply to a business in India.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Who else sees it

Only the providers needed to do the job: Cloudflare and hosting accounts for deployment, the registrar for your domain, Google Fonts for font delivery, and your email, WhatsApp or Instagram provider for the conversation. Each of them handles your data under their own terms. We do not share your content with anyone else.

5. How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become projects are deleted within 12 months. For active and past projects, we keep your contact details and project files for as long as we might support the site, plus the period required by Indian tax and accounting rules. You can ask us to delete anything that is not legally required to be kept.

6. Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email ajlanabduljlaeel@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. You can also complain to the relevant data protection authority if you are not satisfied with our response.

7. Your content and ownership

You keep every right to the content you send us. We use it only to build and support your site, and we may show your project in our portfolio unless you ask us not to. We never claim ownership of your name, photos, work or domain.

8. Cookies

This website sets no cookies of its own. Your browser or email app may store technical data, and Google Fonts may set its own, but nothing on this site is used to identify you across other websites.

9. Security

Your site is served over HTTPS. We send and receive messages through encrypted channels, keep access credentials out of shared documents, and remove access when a project ends. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so please do not send passwords or card details by message.

10. Children

This service is aimed at adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18; if you believe a minor has contacted us, tell us and we will delete the conversation.

11. Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page. Continuing to use the service after a change means the updated version applies.
