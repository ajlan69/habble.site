Every project begins with a written scope: the pages or sections included, the features listed for the chosen package, the delivery date and the price. Anything not listed in that scope is not included and is quoted separately before it is started.

2. Payment

Payment happens in two halves. Nothing is charged before you have approved a written scope — once you approve it, the 50% advance starts the work, and the remaining 50% is due when the site is delivered and live. Work is paused if an invoice is outstanding for more than 7 days after a written reminder.

Quotes are valid for 14 days. Third-party costs — domain registration, hosting, paid plugins or stock assets — are billed to you directly by the provider and are non-refundable once billed.

Delays in receiving content move the date accordingly.
