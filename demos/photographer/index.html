About

One long monsoon,
and a lot of patience.

I'm Aravind — born in Thrissur, based in Fort Kochi. I picked up my first camera at seventeen during a Theyyam festival in Kannur and never really put it down.

My work moves between the ceremonial and the everyday: a bride's hands in a Thrissur courtyard, the slow arc of a fishing net at dusk, the way mist settles into the tea slopes above Munnar. I shoot quietly, mostly with available light, and I like leaving room for things to happen.

Available for weddings across Kerala, editorial assignments, and portrait sessions wherever the light is good.

— Aravind
