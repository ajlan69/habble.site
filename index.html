Services

What’s included in a typical project.

The scope of a standard website build — so you know exactly what you’re getting.

  • Custom visual design — built around your business, not a template.
  • Mobile & desktop responsive — works well on every phone and screen.
  • Business details & services — your work, prices and opening hours presented clearly.
  • Contact & enquiry links — WhatsApp, call or email from anywhere on the site.
  • Basic on-page SEO — titles, descriptions and structure search engines understand.

FAQ

Quick answers.

What type of businesses do you build websites for?

Shops and retail, cafés and restaurants, salons and beauty businesses, local services, consultants and growing small businesses across Kerala and India.

How much does a website cost?

From ₹2,999 for a one-page starter site. Most businesses choose Business at ₹5,999. A clear written quote is always provided before work begins.

How long does it take?

Starter sites target 3–5 working days, Business 5–7 and Business Plus 7–12 — once we receive your content and approval.

Do I own my domain?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name and paid to the registrar. We never take ownership of a client domain.

Is hosting included?

Hosting is a separate recurring cost that you control. We help you set it up in your own account so it stays yours.

Can I request additional features?

Yes. Extra pages, online booking, e-commerce or other features are scoped and quoted separately before they are built.

Can an existing website be redesigned?

Yes. Send us your current site and we’ll quote a redesign that keeps what works and improves the rest.

What information should I provide to get started?

Just a short note about your business — what you do, where you are and what you need the website to do. We’ll take it from there.
