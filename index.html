FAQ

Quick answers.

How quickly can my website be ready?

Starter targets ~48 hours. Professional & Premium vary by scope — agreed in writing before you pay.

Who owns the domain?

You. Bought in your name, paid to the registrar at cost. Never ours.

Where is the website hosted?

Preferably your own free Cloudflare Pages account. If we host it, that’s under a written agreement — and you can move anytime.

How does payment work?

Two halves: 50% after you approve the written scope, 50% when the site is live.

Can you help with the design?

Yes. Send photos, text and links over WhatsApp — the direction is built around your work, not a template.

What happens after launch?

7 days of support are free. Later edits go through an optional ₹500/month plan or a small written quote.
